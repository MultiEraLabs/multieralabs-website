SOFTWARE ACROSS GENERATIONS

About MultiEra Labs

Old computers still work. The modern web often does not.

MISSION

MultiEra Labs develops, tests and documents software, websites and hardware across generations — from 8-bit systems to Apple Silicon.

PRINCIPLES

  • Real compatibility tests instead of assumptions
  • Emulators for speed, real hardware whenever possible
  • No artificial exclusion of old systems
  • Open documentation of limits and results
  • One project, multiple editions: Modern, Legacy and 8-Bit

THREE EDITIONS

Modern · Legacy · 8-Bit

The same project, adapted to very different generations of hardware and browsers.

REAL LAB

The Hardware Lab preserves practical experience with actual machines instead of only listing specifications.
