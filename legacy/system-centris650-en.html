Macintosh Centris 650
The Macintosh Centris 650 arrived in February 1993 and belongs to Apple's short-lived Centris family. With a 25 MHz 68LC040 or 68040, multiple NuBus slots and generous memory expansion, it was a capable business and publishing Mac of the early 1990s.

TECHNICAL DATA
Introduction1993
CPUMotorola 68LC040 or 68040, 25 MHz
Data path32-bit
RAM4 or 8 MB standard; depending on onboard RAM up to 132 or 136 MB
RAM slots4 x 72-pin SIMM
Graphics512 KB VRAM, expandable to 1 MB
StorageSCSI; 80 MB, 230 MB and, depending on configuration, up to 500 MB
OpticalOptional 2x CD-ROM
Expansion3 NuBus slots plus Processor Direct Slot

PERIOD CONFIGURATIONS
The entry configuration could ship with a 68LC040 without an integrated FPU; higher-end configurations used the full 68040. Typical systems had 4 or 8 MB RAM, SCSI hard disks and an optional internal 2x CD-ROM. Four 72-pin SIMM slots made the Centris 650 unusually expandable for its time.
