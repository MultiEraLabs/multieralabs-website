WELCOME TO MULTIERA LABS
Welcome to MultiEra Labs.

We develop, test and document software, web technology and hardware across many computer generations: from 8-bit systems to modern Macs and PCs.

The idea is simple: old computers still work. So we build software and websites that do not automatically leave them behind.
