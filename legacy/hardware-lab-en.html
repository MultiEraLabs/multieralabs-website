HARDWARE LAB - REAL TEST SYSTEMS
The Hardware Lab is about real machines, not only emulators. Our Pentium III development station and four other classic systems are available to us as physical test platforms. We document their historical hardware, typical configurations, original use cases and our present-day MultiEra tests.

Why it matters: emulators are ideal for rapid testing. Original hardware shows how browsers, networks, storage and software behave within the real limitations of their era.

PRIMARY DEVELOPMENT SYSTEM - PENTIUM III
MultiBoot Labs PC - Intel Pentium III, 500 MHz
RAM: approx. 250 MB   Graphics: S3 Savage4
Storage: 16 GB CF-IDE   Network: 3Com
Other equipment: Sound Blaster AWE64, Gotek with FlashFloppy and CRT monitor.

Operating systems: MS-DOS 6.22, Windows for Workgroups 3.11, Windows 95 OSR2 and Windows 98 SE.

We build retro software with Open Watcom here and test MHProbe, MultiEra Archive and MEDirectory directly on real hardware.
