G15Control

macOS application for the Logitech G15 LCD and World of Warcraft integration.

ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

Keep the orange G15 LCD useful on a modern Mac.

G15Control connects the Logitech G15 Refresh to modern macOS. The app provides LCD functions and also acts as the bridge to World of Warcraft data and the G15Control in-game addon.

macOSApple SiliconG15 Refresh160×43 LCD

Use cases

For users who want to use the G15 as a real status display again, not just as a keyboard.

  • Timers and time functions directly on the LCD
  • Game status while playing World of Warcraft
  • Hardware-button control instead of extra desktop windows

Current lab status

Timer, stopwatch and countdown work in the current development build.

Testing currently uses modern macOS/Apple Silicon with a Logitech G15 Refresh and its orange 160×43 LCD.

Target systems

macOSG15 RefreshWoW

The WoW integration covers our development branches for Classic, TBC, WotLK, MoP and Midnight. Feature coverage can vary by game version.

LCD functions

  • Timer, stopwatch and countdown
  • Start, pause, stop and reset states
  • Control through the four LCD buttons
  • M1–M3 profiles as a basis for different views
  • Compact status pages for the 160×43 pixel LCD

World of Warcraft

  • Player data such as name, class, level, health and resources
  • Zone, sub-zone, map and position
  • In-game database for instances, bosses and loot
  • Quest/database functions depending on the supported addon branch
  • The Mac app and in-game addon are developed as one connected system

Planned

These items are not released features yet:

  • Temporary LCD overlays for messages, email and calls
  • Convenient addon installer for supported WoW versions
  • Guide/farming/profession functions in the in-game addon
  • Additional G15 applets and status pages
